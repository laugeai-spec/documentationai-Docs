## Logging In

1. Go to `https://nothing2install.com`
2. Click "Log In" in the top-right corner.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Or use Google or Apple to sign in instantly.
5. Click "Continue"

<Callout kind="info">
Forgot your password? Use "Reset Password" to get a link by email.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting
- Make sure your email is correct
- Check spam for verification emails
