---
title: Stream Not Loading
description: What to do if your game won't start streaming.
---

## Symptoms
- Black screen or never-ending loading
- "Unable to start stream" error

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your connection">
    ````bash
    speedtest
    # Need at least 10 Mbps for smooth streaming
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart your browser">
    Close and reopen your browser; try incognito mode as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable extensions">
    Ad-blockers or privacy extensions can block streaming.
  </Step>
</Steps>
